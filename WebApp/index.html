FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?+

Yes — HTMLStudio is free to use. There's no paid tier, no locked features, and no credit card required. Just create an account and start building.

Where is my data stored?+

In your own browser. Accounts, templates, saved blocks, and UTM presets live in local storage on your device — nothing is sent to a server. That means your work is private by default, but it also doesn't sync between devices.

Will the exported HTML work in Outlook?+

The builder generates classic table-based HTML — the format email clients have rendered reliably for decades — and exports a responsive 600px layout you can paste into any sending platform.

Can I host images for my emails?+

Yes — connect your own ImageKit account and the editor uploads images to your CDN and inserts email-optimized, Outlook-safe image tags in one click. See the repo's Server/imagekit-auth folder for the small auth worker it needs.

Can I use it offline?+

After the first load, the editor works offline — everything it needs runs client-side in the tab.
